### Academic Biography

Toufique Ahammad Gazi is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at the School of Engineering & Technology (SOET), Adamas University, Kolkata, India. With an extensive background in both core computing systems and applied intelligence, his work focuses on advancing cyber defense architectures, IoT/cloud network security, and real-time computer vision systems.

He has contributed peer-reviewed research papers and scholarly book chapters with leading international academic publishers including IGI Global. His current research investigations center on cyber threat intelligence, exploit kit dissection, cyber kill chain analysis, and multitask transfer learning for heterogeneous multimodal data streams.

At Adamas University, he is actively engaged in undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, curriculum development, laboratory instruction, and guiding capstone research projects.
