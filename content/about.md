### Faculty Profile & Academic Mission

Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at **Adamas University, Kolkata**. My academic work converges at the crossroads of **Cybersecurity & Threat Intelligence**, **Cloud/IoT Network Observability**, and **Applied Computer Vision**.

Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering at the **University of Engineering & Management (UEM)**. My research focuses on exploit kit analysis, zero-day threat containment via the Cyber Kill Chain, and deep multitask transfer learning for heterogeneous data streams. In the classroom, I am dedicated to cultivating deep systems intuition and ethical problem-solving among engineering students.
